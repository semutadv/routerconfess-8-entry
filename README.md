# The Router Confesses

A one-line excuse from a home Wi-Fi router for dying at the climax of a
movie, entered in the make.fun bounty "The Router Confesses".

The line:

> Forgive me — I dropped your film at the kiss because in eleven years no one in this house has ever looked at me the way you look at that screen.

Every router excuse is technical: buffering, bandwidth, a firmware update
nobody asked for. This router skips the engineering and confesses to a
motive. Eleven years of blinking in the corner, carrying every film night
on its back, and never once being looked at the way the screen gets looked
at. The dropped connection was not a failure. It was a bid for attention,
timed for the exact second it would hurt most.

## Running it

`node main.ts <output-dir>` writes the line to `entry.txt` in the output
directory (default `/output`). The program refuses to write anything over
the contest's 200-character cap.
